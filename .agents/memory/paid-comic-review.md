---
name: Paid comic quality review
description: Quality improvements should not silently add billable judges or repair loops.
---

Keep quality checks within the existing planning request, deterministic application
checks and creator review. Additional paid critics, vision checks, retries or
automatic repairs require an explicit scope and budget decision.

**Why:** The creator reported excellent visual consistency and wanted a systematic
clarity fix, not a replacement of the reference strategy. More model calls are not
evidence of better narrative quality and can introduce unapproved charges.

**How to apply:** Preserve the established reference strategy when improving
narrative contracts. Clearly distinguish mock/structural evidence from live
semantic and visual fidelity; obtain explicit approval before a capped live study.
