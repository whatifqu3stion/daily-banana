The user wants systematic fixes across all settings variations and a specific understanding of every phase of the comic process, rather than assuming that a poor story prompt caused the problem.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked whether the issue came from the story prompt or elsewhere and said, “We need to be specific and understand all phases of our complex process.”

**How to apply:** Trace the settings and story intent through the complete process and its alternate paths before choosing a fix. Distinguish observed output, user corrections, code evidence and reproduced causes; do not generalize a single style's remedy to every setting.
